Job & Recruitment Safety

Protect yourself when responding to work opportunities. Be cautious if someone asks for money upfront, passwords, PINs, two-step verification codes, banking login details or unnecessary identity information.

Do not send sensitive identity documents simply because someone claims to be offering work. Check who you are dealing with and whether the information requested is reasonably required.

If a job, employer or message connected with JobsNZ appears suspicious, please report it to JobsNZ so it can be reviewed.

If you believe you have been the victim of fraud, identity theft or another crime, contact the appropriate New Zealand authorities.
