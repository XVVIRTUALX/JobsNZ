Add Your Industry to JobsNZ

JobsNZ is a modular workforce platform designed to host multiple industry-specific job and workforce tools under a shared public framework.

We welcome expressions of interest from industries, organisations, collectives, and service providers who wish to contribute an industry-specific application, tool, or pathway that supports lawful work opportunities in Aotearoa / New Zealand.

Who this is for

  • Industry groups or associations
  • Small business collectives
  • Sector-led workforce initiatives
  • Community or regional employment programmes
  • Project-based or seasonal industries
