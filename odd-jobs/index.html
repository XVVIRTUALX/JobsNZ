Add Your Industry to JobsNZ

JobsNZ is a modular workforce platform designed to host multiple industry-specific job and workforce tools under a shared public framework.

We welcome expressions of interest from industries, organisations, collectives, and service providers who wish to contribute an industry-specific application, tool, or pathway that supports lawful work opportunities in Aotearoa / New Zealand.

What “Adding an Industry” Means

Adding an industry to JobsNZ does not create an employment relationship, endorsement, or formal affiliation. Each industry application operates independently, with its own responsibilities, content, and compliance obligations.

JobsNZ provides a shared platform structure, visibility, and a consistent public-facing experience, while allowing each industry to define how its workforce tools or job pathways operate.

Who This Is For

  • Industry groups or associations
  • Small business collectives
  • Sector-led workforce initiatives
  • Community or regional employment programmes
  • Project-based or seasonal industries

What We Look For

  • Alignment with lawful employment in New Zealand
  • Clear industry or sector purpose
  • Practical benefit for workers and employers
  • Willingness to operate transparently and responsibly

How to Express Interest

If you are interested in adding your industry or contributing an industry-specific tool to JobsNZ, please get in touch with a brief outline covering:

  • Your industry or sector
  • The type of workforce tool or job pathway you are proposing
  • The audience it is intended to serve
  • A contact person or organisation

Enquiries can be sent to the Virtual Online Contact Centre:

Email: virtualonlinenz@outlook.com

We will respond to discuss suitability, scope, and next steps.
