Job Search Activity Report — Optional

Workers have told us they would like an easy way to keep a record of the jobs they have applied for.

JobsNZ can create a personal Job Search Activity Report showing applications you make through JobsNZ, including the job, date of application and application status.

You can use or share your report whenever you choose.

Private and optional:
This service does not affect your JobsNZ profile or your ability to apply for work. Your Job Search Activity Report is not displayed to employers.

Your job-search report is for you. JobsNZ does not use this feature to monitor whether you are applying for enough jobs.
