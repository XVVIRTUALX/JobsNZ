JobsNZ Privacy Statement

JobsNZ is operated by PutaiaoTech under the management of Virtual Online Contact Centre (VOCC). JobsNZ respects the privacy of workers, employers, businesses and other users of the Platform.

Information We Collect

Depending on how you use JobsNZ, we may collect information such as:

  • Name and contact information
  • Worker profile and work preference information
  • Employer or business information
  • Job listings and applications or expressions of interest
  • Industry membership information
  • Enquiries and complaints
  • Platform administration, moderation and review records

Information About Other People

If you provide JobsNZ with personal information about another person, for example when making an enquiry or complaint, you should only provide information that is relevant and reasonably necessary for the matter being reported.

JobsNZ may review, restrict access to, retain, or remove information where necessary to protect privacy, safety, platform integrity, or to comply with legal obligations.

Why We Collect Information

JobsNZ collects information where reasonably necessary to operate, administer and protect the Platform, including connecting workers with work opportunities, administering memberships, moderating listings and profiles, responding to enquiries and complaints, maintaining platform security and complying with legal obligations.

Worker Information

Information submitted through a worker profile is stored for JobsNZ administration and moderation. Private contact information and other information designated as private is not intended to be made publicly available.

Where JobsNZ later provides public worker profiles, only information approved or designated for public display will be published.

Enquiries and Complaints

Information supplied through the JobsNZ Enquiries & Complaints process may be reviewed by authorised VOCC personnel for the purpose of responding to the matter, protecting users and the Platform, and determining whether platform action or referral to another process may be appropriate.

A complaint or allegation is not treated as a proven finding merely because it has been submitted to JobsNZ.

Information Security

JobsNZ uses administrative and technical controls intended to protect information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or loss. Access to private administrative information is restricted to authorised users.

Disclosure of Information

JobsNZ will not intentionally disclose private personal information except where reasonably necessary to provide the Platform or service, where the person has authorised disclosure, or where disclosure is required or permitted by New Zealand law.

Retention

JobsNZ will retain personal information only for as long as it is reasonably required for the purpose for which it was collected, legitimate platform administration, record keeping, dispute handling, security, or legal obligations.

Information that is no longer reasonably required may be deleted, anonymised or securely archived where appropriate.

Accuracy and Access

Users may contact JobsNZ to request correction of personal information held about them or to ask about access to their personal information, subject to applicable New Zealand law.

Third-Party Services

JobsNZ may use third-party technology or service providers to support platform operation, hosting, payments, communications or administration. Those services may process information where necessary to provide their services.

New Zealand Privacy Law

JobsNZ intends to manage personal information consistently with the Privacy Act 2020 and applicable New Zealand privacy requirements.

Contact

Privacy enquiries or concerns may be submitted through the JobsNZ Enquiries & Complaints process.
