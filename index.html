New Zealand Employment Information

JobsNZ connects people with work opportunities and service providers. Employers, workers and contractors remain responsible for understanding and complying with their obligations under New Zealand law.

Employment New Zealand — Employment Rights & Responsibilities

Employment New Zealand — Employee or Contractor?

WorkSafe New Zealand — Health & Safety Rights and Obligations

JobsNZ provides a platform for connecting people with work opportunities and service providers. Registration, job listings, applications, enquiries or communications through JobsNZ do not constitute an offer, promise or guarantee of employment or work.

Anyone registering as a worker must be currently entitled to work in New Zealand. Worker identity and work eligibility may be subject to verification before a profile is approved or published.

Work arrangements are made directly between users. Users are responsible for ensuring they are legally entitled to undertake the work offered and for complying with applicable New Zealand employment, contracting, tax, health and safety, and other legal requirements.
