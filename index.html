JobsNZ is a New Zealand job-matching and workforce coordination platform managed through VOCC, the business management and operations division of PūtaiaoTech.

JobsNZ is designed for New Zealand citizens and people currently living in New Zealand who have the legal right to work in New Zealand. JobsNZ is not an immigration, visa, overseas recruitment or employment-guarantee service and does not promise or guarantee employment.

Work arrangements are made directly between users. Users are responsible for ensuring they are legally entitled to undertake the work offered and for complying with applicable New Zealand employment, contracting, tax, health and safety, and other legal requirements.
