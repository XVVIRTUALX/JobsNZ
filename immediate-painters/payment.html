Emerging Digital Payment Infrastructure

JobsNZ is exploring future-ready payment systems to support faster, secure, and traceable transactions across our platform.

One developing framework of interest is Project Babbage (BRC100) — a digital infrastructure initiative focused on enabling scalable internet-native payments and data exchange.

This work is associated with developments within the BSV (Bitcoin SV) ecosystem, including a protocol stabilisation upgrade known as “Chronicles”, aimed at permanently locking the original Bitcoin protocol structure for long-term enterprise reliability.

  • Secure micro-payments for job deposits and quotes
  • Immutable contract and work completion records
  • Data-linked payment verification
  • Cross-border contractor payments

These systems are currently under observation and development and are not yet the primary payment method on JobsNZ.
